Extreme high-angle wide shot from near the ceiling of an enormous Cantonese wedding-style banquet hall at night. Ten round banquet tables with white tablecloths and glass lazy susans are arranged in a grid, and every seat is filled: about a hundred copies of the same young man, each in a different wild costume: pirates, wizards with owls, astronauts in plain white spacesuits, cowboys, knights in armor, chefs, firefighters, grandpas, grown men in baby bonnets in giant high chairs, gym bros, CEOs in suits, ballerinas, clowns, scuba divers. All are laughing, eating and toasting. Giant crystal chandeliers hang in the top of the frame; red-and-gold walls, red lanterns, red lacquer columns, a small stage with red curtains. The table at the center-bottom of the frame is the original table from image 1, with the same eleven people and the real one in the light-blue hoodie on its far side. 18mm, deep focus, haze.

Photorealistic cinematic film still, ARRI Alexa 35, Kodak Vision3 500T grade, warm tungsten practicals, light haze. No text, no signage lettering, no characters or symbols on the walls, stage or banners, no logos, no watermark.
